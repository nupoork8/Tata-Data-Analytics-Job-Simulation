# Tata Group – Virtual Data Analytics Job Simulation (AI & Credit Risk)

This repository contains my work from the **Tata Group AI-Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation** hosted on Forage.  
The simulation involved real-world applications of Generative AI and data strategy for the Financial Services team at **Tata iQ**.

---

## 🧠 Overview
- **Platform:** Forage
- **Company:** Tata Group (Tata iQ)
- **Domain:** Data Analytics, AI & Credit Risk
- **Tools & Tech:** GenAI Tools, Agentic AI, No-code Predictive Modeling
- **Completion Date:** January 2026

---

## 🛠 Tasks Completed

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Data Quality
- Conducted EDA using **GenAI tools** to assess data quality and integrity.
- Identified critical risk indicators and structured data insights to prepare for predictive modeling.

### 2. No-Code Predictive Modeling
- Proposed and justified a **no-code predictive modeling framework** to assess customer delinquency risk.
- Leveraged GenAI to develop structured model logic and define rigorous evaluation criteria.

### 3. AI-Driven Strategy & Automation
- Designed a comprehensive collections strategy leveraging **Agentic AI** and automation.
- Incorporated **Ethical AI principles**, regulatory compliance, and scalable implementation frameworks into the final design.
